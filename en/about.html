About me

DESIGNING FROM WHO I AM

Scroll

Something about nostalgia seduces me. We grow up developing a love for things; for grandma's sewing machine, dad's watch, the marks on the wall showing how much we grew each year.

We become adults and end up keeping a potpourri of disconnected fragments that remind us of what we love. We combine the identity we inherited with the one we build, and they don't always get along. Maybe that is why, sometimes, the spaces we live in don't quite know who we are either.

Architect and Site Foreman graduated from the National University of Córdoba. Trained in Bioarchitecture at UTN, in BIM methodology and virtual reality with Autodesk Academic Partners certification, and specialized in Furniture Design at FAUD.

My name is Catalina. I am an architect, but I consider myself an artist.

I always struggled to see space as walls and nothing more. For me, places are not the things, but the spark that makes us feel they are ours.

It is not about choosing which vase you like best, but about being able to look around and see a little piece of yourself in every corner.

I won't impose any trend on you. I will help you blend all the fragments that make you who you are and bring them into your space.

I want to know who you are, and above all for you to know it too. When you are clear about what defines you, you no longer doubt how you want to live.
