Frequently asked questions

It is for you if you only need to solve one piece of custom furniture for your space. It does not apply if you need to rethink how the space works, redecorate, choose wall paint, carry out construction work or change finishes.

Just fill in the survey form to send us photos and measurements of where the furniture will go. We guide you through taking the measurements, but you will need a tape measure for this step.

In Córdoba City and surrounding areas, yes. We can build and install your furniture as an additional service. If you live in another city, we give you the assembly instructions so any trusted carpenter can build the design independently and without mistakes.

We design one piece per consultation (or one set, like a fitted kitchen) to ensure the precision of every technical detail. If you need more than one, we handle each order separately.

The timeline is set in the quote based on the complexity of the furniture, although the estimated delivery time is one week.
